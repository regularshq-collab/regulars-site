02 / Not included

What we
don't do

You will get pitched all of this by somebody. It is not what we sell, and we would rather say so now than have you find out in month two.

— Paid ad management We don't run Google or Facebook ads
— Websites We don't build or host your site
— Fake or filtered reviews We ask. We never write, buy, or hide them
— Answering live calls We handle the ones you miss, by text
— Cold outreach We only contact people who contacted you

Why a setup fee

The first week is real work and it is done by a person, not a signup form. We connect your number and calendar, learn your prices and your service area, write the messages in your voice, and test the whole thing before a single customer sees it. The $500 covers that. It is charged once and never again.

Why 90 days

Comeback campaigns and review requests take a couple of months to show up in your numbers. Ninety days is the shortest honest window to judge it on. After that there is no contract at all — you tell us to stop and we stop at the end of that month.
