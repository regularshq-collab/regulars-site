Privacy,
in plain English

No dark patterns, no thousand-word hedge. Here is exactly what this website does, what happens if you get in touch, and how we treat a customer list once it is ours to run.

We use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views and see which pages people read. It sets no cookies and does not build a profile of you or follow you to other websites. We cannot tell who you are from it.

Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which means Google's servers see your IP address when the font file loads. That is standard for most of the web, but you should know it happens. Everything else on the page — every style, script, and graphic — is served from this site directly.

02 / Getting in touch

If you book
or message us

The contact form

It does not send anything to us on its own. When you press the button, it opens your own email app with the message already written, and nothing leaves your device until you press send yourself.

Once you do send it, we have whatever you wrote — your name, business, trade, email, phone if you gave one — sitting in an ordinary email inbox. We use it to reply to you and nothing else.

Booking a call

The booking button hands you to Calendly, which is a separate company with its own privacy policy. Whatever you type into their form is held by them and passed to us so we know who we are meeting.

We keep those details to run the call and follow up. If you tell us you are not interested, we stop.

Your customer list stays yours. We run it. We never own it.

— The part that matters most

If you become a client, we handle your customers' names, phone numbers, emails, and job history so that Capture, ReviewPing and Comeback can do their work. We are processing that information on your behalf, under your instructions.

We do not sell it. We do not rent it. We do not market to your customers for anybody else, and we never mix your list into anyone else's. If you cancel, you get an export and we stop using it.

Every Comeback email carries a one-click unsubscribe, and we honour it immediately. Anyone who replies asking to be left alone is removed.
